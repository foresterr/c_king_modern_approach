It's the same in most cases, the difference is that the sign (if present) counts as one of the
characters that need to fit into minimum field width, precision specifies minimum number of digits,
not characters.

So -1 printed with %04d will be -001 - that's 4 characters total.
But -1 printed with %.4d is -0001 - that's 4 digits (the sign is not a digit)
