char *p = "abc";

(a) putchar(p); invalid, putchar expects a char, not pointer to char (would probably print garbage)
(b) putchar(*p); valid, prints "a" - p by itself points to first character of "abc\0"
(c) puts(p); valid, prints "abc"
(d) puts(*p); invalid, puts expect pointer to char, but *p is just a char (would probably refer to invalid memory location with horrible consequences)
