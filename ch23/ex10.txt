Modified:
if (strstr("foo\1bar\1baz", str) != NULL)

It does assume that the separator character does not occur in string being searched for (e.g.
"foo\1b" would get a match), and also that str cannot be a substring of one of the strings being
searched for (e.g. "oo" would also get a match)
